## Thesis
Qualified coaches may use verified LaneSight Shot/Form evidence to benchmark students and evaluate progress. Avoid implying formal coach certification, automatic diagnostic correctness or availability of metrics still gated.

## MVP/Beta positioning
Coaches are discovery partners and reviewers, **not** proof that a paid coach portal, custom benchmark tools or premium tiers are already launched. No sensor integration in MVP/friends Beta (DEC-070).

## Pilot proposal
Owner recruits a small, flexible set of coaches (count OPEN) to evaluate capture guidance, clinically precise technique annotations, useful angle/timing reports, privacy controls, per-session notes and human coaching judgement. Use a written pilot permission and feedback route via the one `feedback` table.

## Potential product requirements (future)
Coach/student consent linkage, private benchmarks versioned by coach, history comparisons, annotations, access revocation, export scopes and coach training. Restrict coach visibility to explicitly shared records.

## Commercial alignment
Coaching price and allowances must use **the same** T0/T1/T2 tier decision matrix in `../01-business/pricing-financial-strategy.md`; all names, monthly shot allowances, Form allowances and coach fees remain OPEN. Never publish separate coaching tiers.

## Validation metrics
Student repeat rate, coach time saved, student understanding, benchmark reliability, privacy issues, technical/support cost per coaching account.
