## Targets
Authorized scoring vendors, center operators, coach organizations, manufacturers, leagues and industry creators. Build a prioritization log: value, access rights, data quality, engineering burden, privacy impact, monetization option and contractual constraint.

## Sequencing
In MVP and friends Beta, integrations are **not dependencies**. Internal paired-sensor comparison for research only; no sensor features, sensor picker or vendor access promise (DEC-070). Future scoring feeds require permission and provenance, and cannot silently change official records.

## Outreach artifact
Brief explanation of product, requested read-only fields (when relevant), data use/security architecture, retention/data rights, test environment, expected volume, commercial terms and point of contact.

## Acceptance checklist
Written API/data rights, scoped token, retention/deletion mapping, terms compliance, latency and quota model, user consent, field mappings, graceful fallback and offboarding plan.

## Priority proposal
Coach validation first; scoring/center pilots later only if lawful and high-value; hardware vendors deferred to post-MVP explicit DEC.
