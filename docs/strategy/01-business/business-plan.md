## Business model hypothesis
Video-based shot/Form performance intelligence with durable bowler history. Free entry may improve acquisition; subscriptions and coaching-oriented services are *hypotheses*, not approved offers. Avoid hardware inventory and center software obligations in early stages.

## Customer problems and positioning
Bowling information is fragmented across scores, videos, balls, league apps and coaching notes. LaneSight's first job is to produce trustworthy metrics and actionable improvement from ordinary phone captures. Make explicit the gap between observed and inferred values.

## Segments
1. League bowler seeking practice consistency; 2. competitive tournament bowler tracking technique; 3. independent coach seeking repeatable evidence. Interview all three before paid packaging.

## Distribution tests
Founder demonstrations, coach referrals, community education, then a permission-based waitlist. Analyze conversion source → activation → weekly return → willingness to pay. Never claim PBA/USBC/vendor affiliation absent permission.

## Major dependencies
Near-field video and Form reliability, customer capture education, low-cost processing, App Store account-deletion/privacy obligations, user support capacity. Record competitive integration dependencies as unverified, never blocking founder MVP.

## Four testable business hypotheses
- People will record multiple shots when setup is simple.
- Trustworthy partial measurements outperform impressive but wrong full-lane measurements.
- Repeat historical trends are the reason to return.
- Coaches can increase retention without bespoke enterprise infrastructure.

## Evidence needed
Track 5–10 founder-observed sessions, friend interviews, per-shot utility ratings, repeated-use intent, willingness-to-pay interviews and measured service costs. These are proposed research samples, **not a Beta-size decision**.
