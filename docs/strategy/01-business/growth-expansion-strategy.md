## Expansion ladder
Founder proof → repeat individual practice → optional coach-assisted practice → team/community invitation → authorized records/integrations → future league/center product. These are staged hypotheses, not promises.

## Sequencing guardrails
Do not scale acquisition while first-session completion, accuracy and cost are unknown. Do not market far-lane metrics or sensors before later decisions. Avoid B2B enterprise roadmaps that divert from trusted shot/Form value.

## Experiments
Test friend referral requests, coach demonstration cohorts, partner bowling-center demos with written permission, shareable non-video insight summaries, and educational content. Use defined funnel denominator and weekly observation periods.

## Scale signals
Repeat active practice, declining support effort/user, positive coaching utility, stable analysis outcome distributions and measured gross margin before widening invitations.

## Partnerships dependency
Any scoring-provider or pro-association integration requires explicit authorization; no discovery response is a technical or legal integration grant.
