## Status and scope
A planning checklist, not legal advice, not an assertion that LLC, EIN, trademark or patent clearance is complete. **Friends-Beta legal P0 set is OPEN** and must be decided with competent counsel where needed.

## Candidate pre-invite review
Business entity/registered agent/EIN if required for contracting and tax; website/domain control and trademark search; app Terms, Privacy Policy and account deletion implementation; Apple App Privacy disclosures; retention language for key-frame media and retained de-identified feedback; child age gate and under-13 flow (DEC-063 staged under_13 test; do not onboard children in Beta absent compliant plan); incident-response contacts and breach escalation; capture consent and rights when other bowlers may appear; coach/vendor agreements and footage usage permissions.

## Particular design facts to disclose accurately
Analyze uploaded/live clip transiently then delete; **do not retain clips**. Keep permitted Form key frames and profile avatars. Feedback is retained in one table and can remain de-identified after account deletion (DEC-059). Avoid claiming absolute anonymity without verifying free text/diagnostics scrub logic. No automatic sensor access for customers.

## IP workstream
Conduct clearance for name/mark and app icon, document first-use/provenance and contributor IP assignments, record inventions with dates and prior-art assessment. A patent is a separate counsel-led ROI decision, not required by default.

## Permission gates
No unlicensed manufacturer photo hosting, use links where authorized; no scoring, sensor, USBC or league data integrations without access rights; avoid representing unofficial data as certified.

## Exit artifacts
Owner-approved launch legal checklist, counsel questions log, verified policy URLs and version dates, confirmed provider and deletion controls. None automatically gates founder MVP; friends-Beta P0 scope awaits explicit founder selection.
