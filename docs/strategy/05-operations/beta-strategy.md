## Phase contract
Founder-only MVP continues until Shayne calls its end (DEC-047). Friends Beta only when the owner chooses; **size, waves, recruitment criteria and incentives are OPEN**. Prior proposed 60 participants/three waves is a future option, not default.

## Suggested tester diversity (not a numerical decision)
Mix bowling skill, phone models, sides/rear camera positions, lighting, different lane finishes and first-time onboarding skill. Restrict staged testing accounts to QA and exclude from feedback/behavior KPIs (DEC-063).

## Research & support plan
Explicit consent and privacy disclosures; guide correct capture; analyze quality/failure coverage, session depth, satisfaction, app crashes and cost per completed shot. All structured feedback uses the retained `feedback` table (DEC-059), tagged phase/version. Owner triages and can request reproduction without a permanent clip archive.

## Readiness evidence, not automatic gates
View-specific validated metric error, honest unavailability, first-shot activation, no cross-account exposure, account deletion, incident recovery, cost compliance and user trust. DEC-043 latency numbers are target state and must not block founder/Beta testing as mandatory benchmarks.

## Rewards
Possible extended access, discounted future paid tier, direct product input or public recognition **only with owner approval and disclosure**; avoid making promises before pricing is decided.

## Timeline
Flexible founder-controlled testing. Review weekly; do not lock 2/4/8-week Beta without owner decision. Exit by owner judgment using evidence packet.
