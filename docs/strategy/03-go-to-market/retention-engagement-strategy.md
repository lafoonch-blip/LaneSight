## Retention thesis
Bowlers return when the same practice routine shows an observable change or coaching benefit. Avoid optimizing push volume instead of improvement.

## Initial loops
Practice session → credible measured/form insight → compare own history → next-session goal → new practice session. Coach-assisted comparison requires explicit consent and coach access controls.

## Lifecycle experiments
First-session setup coaching; one low-frequency progress summary; a reminder chosen by user; practical Form technique task. No public performance leaderboard, team analytics or social sharing by default.

## Key measures
Second-session rate, weekly active practice users, shots/active practice user, high-quality result coverage, 4-week retention, unsubscribes and complaint ratio. Define outlier/staged exclusions.

## Guardrail
Do not invent Improvement Score; one transparent overall LaneSight Score remains the product direction, with provisional/Beta labeling where DEC requires. No fake acceleration of score trends.
