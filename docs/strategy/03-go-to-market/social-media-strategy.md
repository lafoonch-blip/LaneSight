## Platform roles
YouTube: educational demos, technique comparisons and trustworthy explanations. Reddit: participate transparently and answer genuine problems within community rules. Instagram/TikTok: short actionable Form guidance and beta learning. Email/waitlist: permissioned launch announcements.

## Content pillars
How to set up a camera; near-field board reading; what a metric can/cannot say; practice routines; Form timing and angles; design/build transparency; anonymous feedback-led improvements.

## Workflow
Editorial calendar: proposed weekly long-form topic + 2–3 repurposed clips subject to available time. Obtain explicit permission for filming, coaches and participants. Label simulated demos; never create bogus testimonials or commercial affiliations.

## Automated agents
Research/draft assistance only; owner approval before posting, compliance with APIs and community terms. No stealth bots to repeatedly seed promotion.

## Measurement
Qualified waitlist signups, activation by source, returning engaged users, coach inbound contacts and content-to-activation ratio; no assumed conversion targets.
