## Rollout reality
MVP is founder-only. Friends Beta population and waves remain open; market launch is later. No fixed three-wave / 60-user commitment. Owner approval alone transitions phases (DEC-047).

## Customer promise
Understand and improve your bowling with practical video-first Shot and Form feedback. Disclose best-effort metrics and missing readings; do not advertise accurate breakpoint, entry or late speed while Phase-2 gate is open. No consumer sensor feature claims.

## Target customers
Serious league and amateur competitive bowlers; coaches as subsequent referral channel. Need research-backed message testing before spend.

## Funnel
Awareness → waitlist/permission → invitation → signup → first successful analyzed shot/Form clip → second practice session → 4-week meaningful activity → willingness to pay. Define exact event IDs in KPI strategy; track denominators and exclusions.

## Channel experiments
Educational YouTube clips with consent; Reddit replies that obey community self-promotion rules; coaching demos; permission-based local center pilots. Do not use posting bots for undisclosed spam. Track tagged referral sources and signup quality.

## Launch dependencies
Consent/privacy copy, delete/data handling, support channel, instrumentation and owner-reviewed cost/quality. Build a launch-day rollback plan and status communication draft.

## Success definition
Customer trust, active practice and evidence-based readiness rather than downloads, follower counts or hypothetical launch date.
