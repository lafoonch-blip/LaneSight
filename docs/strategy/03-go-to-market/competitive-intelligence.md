## Scope
Maintain dated competitor cards for LaneTalk, scoring and league platforms, shot-tracking solutions, coaching tools and emerging video/sensor entrants. Treat feature claims as unverified until sourced from official materials or firsthand trials.

## Monthly card
Name, customer, core job, pricing evidence/date, metric provenance, interoperability, privacy/data ownership, traction signals with confidence, actionable differentiation, legal/IP notes.

## Opportunity ranking
Rank by user impact × strategic relevance × implementability ÷ cost/risk. Examples: shot-level credibility, simple practice capture, consolidated per-ball trend, coach benchmarking. Do not pursue copycat features solely because a competitor advertises them.

## Ethical guardrails
No reverse engineering protected systems in violation of terms, scraping restricted credentials, or presenting unofficial scoring feeds as licensed.

## Outputs
Monthly differentiation memo, product risks, pricing watch, and 3 experiments with owner decision.
