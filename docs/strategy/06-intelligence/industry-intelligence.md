# Bowling Industry Intelligence Strategy
Status: PROPOSED
Version: 1.1
Last Updated: 2026-10-26

## Sources and insights
Weekly PBA/PWBA/USBC/BPAA, bowling media, major manufacturers, coaches, scoring vendors, competitor releases, creator discussions. Every finding: date of event and publication, verifiable source, LaneSight opportunity, outreach right/permission uncertainty, owner action and urgency.

## Task review
Retain daily bowling opportunity watch and both weekly briefs for 4 weeks. No second daily 8 AM operations watch; the ops task is paused pending telemetry. After 4 weeks user decides keep/change/disable; no automatic decision.

## Ethics
No unauthorized scraping/API access, undisclosed posting bots, or representing unverified partnerships as active.
