# LaneSight Current State
Status: CANONICAL  
Version: 1.0  
Last Updated: 2026-09-27  
Owner: Shayne

## Building now
Stable rear-camera Shot Analyzer and the measurement foundation required for the broader platform.

## Current milestone
Automatically reconstruct a bowling-ball trajectory from tripod video and calculate useful, confidence-scored metrics.

## Immediate sequence
1. Stable tripod video + calibration.
2. Automatic ball detection/tracking.
3. Early sensor synchronization/fusion.
4. Free Shot Analyzer + saved history.
5. Bowler Profile + Arsenal.
6. Session Mode / League Mode + Audio Coach.
7. Coaching intelligence.
8. Team Lane Management.
9. Connected external bowling records.
10. League management / center products after bowler traction.

## MVP
- Fixed rear-camera shot analysis
- Lane calibration
- Ball tracking and trajectory
- Speed, launch, arrows, breakpoint, entry and pin result where reliable
- Intended target vs actual
- Confidence/failure handling
- Account + persistent shot history
- Basic Bowler Profile
- Arsenal/current ball
- Optional early sensor fusion

## Not MVP
- Full league management
- USBC membership renewal
- Direct center scoring integrations
- Team Lane Management
- POV/smart-glasses dependency
- Payments/prize funds

## Current blockers / discovery
- Select/obtain first bowling sensor and validate accessible data
- Collect 30-50 controlled tripod shots
- Finalize calibration workflow and acceptance thresholds
- Research official integration paths for USBC, LeaguePals, LaneTalk and scoring systems

## Latest major strategic decision
LaneSight is a bowler-first platform. Computer vision is the initial acquisition and intelligence wedge, not the entire product. The persistent Bowler Profile is the durable center of the ecosystem.
