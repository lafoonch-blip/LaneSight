## Strategic competitors
- LeaguePals — bowling-specific league administration, payments and center workflow.
- LaneTalk — scoring connectivity, consumer statistics and expanding league-management capability.
- CDE BLS + LeagueSecretary — mature incumbent league-management and bowler-results ecosystem.

## LaneSight differentiation thesis
Existing systems are strongest at results, scoring or administration. LaneSight should connect three layers:
1. Results — scores, averages, leagues, standings.
2. Performance — speed, RPM, accuracy, trajectory, arsenal.
3. Intelligence — why performance changed and what adjustment helped.
