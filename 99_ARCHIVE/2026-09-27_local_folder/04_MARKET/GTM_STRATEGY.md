## Wedge
Free/low-friction Shot Analyzer for serious league/tournament bowlers and coaches.

## Adoption ladder
Shot Analyzer → saved Bowler Profile → Arsenal/Sensor → League Mode → Audio Coach → Team Invite → Team Lane Management → connected league/scoring records → secretary/center pull.

## Network effect
One user gets coaching. Five teammates get shared lane intelligence. A league with many LaneSight users creates a natural reason for the secretary/center to integrate.

## Market-entry rule
Do not start by asking centers to replace their current league software. First create consumer pull and measurable bowler value.

## Distribution candidates
- shareable annotated shot replays
- teammates/team invites
- coaches/pro shops
- tournament bowlers
- sensor partnerships
- center integrations after traction
