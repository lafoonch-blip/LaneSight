Do not lock pricing before engagement and willingness-to-pay testing.

## Possible structure
- **Free:** limited analyses, basic profile/arsenal, basic league companion.
- **Pro:** deeper metrics, session history, coaching, trends and arsenal intelligence.
- **Team:** shared lane intelligence and team strategy.
- **Coach:** multi-athlete review/dashboard.
- **League/Center:** administration, scoring integrations, communications and eventually payments.

Earlier directional hypotheses placed Pro around the high-single-digit/low-double-digit monthly range, but packaging should be validated after product engagement is proven.
