## Master brand
**LaneSight**

## Tagline
**See More. Bowl Smarter.**

## Preferred logo
LS Monogram with a subtle bowling-lane/trajectory motif integrated into the S. It replaces the eye-led concept as the preferred platform mark.

## Why
The mark must credibly work for performance software, coaching, team tools, league management, center products, apparel, events and hardware integrations without making LaneSight look like only a camera app.

## Brand architecture
- LaneSight Profile
- LaneSight Live
- LaneSight Coach
- LaneSight Arsenal
- LaneSight Team
- LaneSight Leagues
- LaneSight Centers

## Current color direction
- Midnight Navy — `#0B1320`
- Lane Blue — `#007BFF`
- Electric Blue — `#00D2FF`
- Cool Gray — `#A7B0BE`
- Ice White — `#F7F9FC`

These are concept values. Finalize accessible screen colors and CMYK/Pantone equivalents when the vector master is produced.

## Usage
Maintain primary horizontal, stacked, mark-only, dark/light and one-color versions. Use the standalone LS mark for app icon, favicon, social avatar and compact surfaces. Avoid making an eye, camera, smart glasses or a literal bowling ball the dominant master-brand symbol.
